# Eco Health Hub (Bio Core) 🌿

An AI-powered mobile application designed to bridge the gap between technology and environmental sustainability. By leveraging cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence, the app empowers users, urban gardeners, and eco-enthusiasts to analyze soil and water quality, show medicinal quality of plant, diagnose plant diseases instantly, and manage smart care routines.

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Smart Plant Scan**: Instantly identify plant species and analyze leaf health or deficiencies using trained model(accuracy=91.26%) and Gemini 2.5 Flash AI.
- **Advanced Soil & Water Analysis**: Input or upload environmental parameters to check suitability for cultivation and drinking/irrigation safety.
- **Automated Care Reminders**: Background notification system to keep track of fertilizer, watering, and disease check schedules.
- **Community Forum**: Community expert or experienced gardeners can help others to reduce AI error in disease scan.
- **Smart Filtering & AI Planner**: Personalized recommendations based on environmental attributes.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Flutter (Dart) - Modern, cross-platform UI.
- **Backend**: FastAPI (Python) - High-performance AI integration layer.
- **AI Engine**: Google Gemini AI (GenAI SDK) - Advanced computer vision & analytical processing.
- **Database**: SQLite & Sqflite - Secure local data and reminder management.
